How to Complete
  1. Complete every shape, carat weight, and quality tier your company can supply on a repeatable basis.
  2. Each row represents one loose single lab-grown diamond specification, not a specific finished setting or matched pair.
  3. For each row, enter the USD price per carat, certification cost per stone, and dropship shipping, insurance, and handling cost per order. State the cost basis and any pricing assumptions in Partner notes.
  4. Do not substitute the prefilled LuxeyDucks quality requirements without written approval.
  5. IGI certification is required for each actual stone supplied. Stone-specific reports, measurements, and media will be reviewed separately before any individual purchase is approved.
  6. After completion, use Print / Save as PDF, then reply to the email that sent you this form with the completed pricing PDF only.
Fixed LuxeyDucks Requirements

Gemstone: Lab-grown diamond.

Luxey Select: F color / VS1 clarity.

Luxey Signature: D-E color / VVS1-2 clarity.

Round Brilliant: Ideal cut grade required, with Excellent symmetry and Excellent polish.

Fancy shapes: Shape-specific ideal proportions, Excellent symmetry, and Excellent polish.

Fluorescence: None.

Certification: IGI required for each actual stone.

The quality tier changes color and clarity. Cut never steps down.

Variable-Weight Pricing Method

Each target carat weight represents a program specification, not one identical stone. Actual stones may vary within the approved tolerance and will have different measurements and final costs.

Please quote a repeatable USD price per carat for every shape, quality tier, and target-weight program. List certification cost per stone and dropship shipping, insurance, and handling cost per order separately; note the cost basis and any assumptions.

Before any individual stone is approved for purchase, LuxeyDucks will review that stone’s exact IGI report, measurements, images or video, current availability, and final cost. No substitution is permitted without LuxeyDucks approval.

Weights: 1.00, 1.50, 2.00, 2.50, 3.00, 4.00, 5.00, and 6.00 ct, using the displayed acceptable weight tolerances.

Shapes: Round Brilliant, Oval, Emerald Cut, Pear, Cushion, Elongated Cushion, Marquise, Radiant, Princess Cut, Asscher, and Heart.
