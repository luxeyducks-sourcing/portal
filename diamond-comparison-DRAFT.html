Hi Adam,

I’ve selected two LuxeyDucks lab-grown emerald-cut diamonds for your ring and compared them with the 3.10 ct Brilliant Earth option you’re considering.

My first choice is the 2.81 ct D/VVS2. Its listed length of 9.83 mm is greater than the 3.10 ct option’s 9.46 mm, despite its lower carat weight. Carat weight measures weight, not visible size. Its approximately 1.47 length-to-width ratio gives it an elongated rectangular outline that I particularly like for this design.

The 2.92 ct E/VVS1 is another candidate if you prefer a longer silhouette. At a listed 9.96 mm long, with an approximately 1.49 ratio, it has the longest and slightly most elongated outline of these three options.

The 3.10 ct D/VS1 option has listed dimensions of 9.46 × 6.75 mm and an approximately 1.40 ratio, giving it a broader rectangular outline. It is the heaviest, but not the longest. That does not make it a poorer choice; it may suit you better if you prefer a wider shape.

I recommend starting with the 2.81 ct D/VVS2 and comparing it visually with the 2.92 ct E/VVS1. Before you decide, we’ll review the available grading reports and videos, confirm pricing and availability, and consider how each stone will work in your intended setting.
